## An independent product

Tainure is the personal project I founded and am currently building end to end.
It connects my experience as a design manager with hands-on product development:
understanding the problem, defining the experience, designing the system and
building the application.

The central idea is to make the context of working relationships useful over
time. Meeting notes become a memory that supports preparation, commitments,
coaching and career conversations.

## Current stage

The product is in private beta. Visit [Tainure](https://tainure.com) to explore
the experience and request an invitation.

They show fictional people and demonstration notes.
