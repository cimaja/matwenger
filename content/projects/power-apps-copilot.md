## Overview

Microsoft 365 Copilot is becoming the place where people do their work, not just a place to ask it questions. As part of a broader shift Microsoft calls [Frontier Transformation](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365/blog/business-leader/2026/03/09/a-new-way-of-working-is-taking-shape-frontier-transformation/), custom Power Apps can now surface directly as agents inside Copilot conversations, powered by the app's own MCP (Model Context Protocol) server. Someone can ask Copilot a plain-language question, like "show me all active candidates in Atlanta," and get back a live, interactive view, a chart or a filtered data grid, generated directly from their app's data, without ever leaving the chat.

The experience works in both directions. Microsoft 365 Copilot and agents like Researcher and Analyst are also available directly inside Power Apps, so someone working in their business app can ask an agent to prioritize clients, analyze account data, or build a pitch deck grounded in the record they have open, without switching tools.

I oversaw the design team responsible for shaping this experience within Power Platform, guiding the direction from early concept through [public preview](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/blog/2026/04/09/whats-new-in-power-platform-april-2026-feature-update/).

## Key responsibilities

- Set design direction for how Power Apps content and interactions surface inside Microsoft 365 Copilot conversations
- Guided the design team through defining visual and interaction patterns for data grids, charts, and custom tools rendered inline in chat
- Partnered with PM and engineering leadership to align the MCP-powered experience with Power Platform's existing design language
- Reviewed and gave feedback across design milestones, from early concept through public preview
- Coached designers on the team as they worked across a genuinely new surface, an AI assistant's chat, not a canvas or a screen

## Impact

- Public preview available starting March 2026, with general availability for model-driven apps in April 2026
- Positions Power Apps among the first business application platforms to appear natively inside Microsoft 365 Copilot conversations, alongside Dynamics 365 Sales and Dynamics 365 Customer Service
- Part of a broader ecosystem push, with the same MCP-powered pattern extending to Microsoft partner apps including Adobe Express, Figma, Canva, and Box
